# Backlog

## Weather and time of day (parked 2026-10-11)

Today: five fixed presets on the Ride tab (Morning, Midday, Golden hour, Nebelmeer valley fog, Alpenglühen), each with its own sun position and cloud cover. Clouds drift and cast shadows; the terrain casts real shadows. No night, rain, snow, overcast sky or live weather, and the sun does not move during a ride.

Planned, in this order:

1. **Real time and live weather** — sun position from the real date, time and location; clouds, wind, rain and temperature from Open-Meteo (free, no API key). Wind acts as a real head- or tailwind in the physics and on the trainer.
2. **Night** — stars and moon, bike headlight lighting the road, lit house windows and street lamps in villages, glowing reflectors.
3. **Rain and overcast** — grey sky, wet dark road reflecting sky and lights, puddles, spray from the tyres, drops on the camera, rain sound.
4. **Snow and seasons** — snow line on the Alpine climbs, morning frost, spring green vs autumn colours.
5. **Time passing** — the sun moves during the ride, so a long ride can start at dusk and end in the dark.
