# Großglockner: art brief

The Großglockner Hochalpenstraße, Salzburg (north) side, from Fusch an der Großglocknerstraße (811 m, km 0) up to the Hochtor. The route is now 26.85 km long. It rides through the whole Hochtor tunnel (26.45 to 26.76 km) and ends about 90 m past the south portal at the Hochtor viewpoint, where a synthetic turning circle turns the rider back. The old end was inside the tunnel at 26.58 km. Facts and sources: `research/grossglockner/visual-reference.md` (note: the route numbers there predate today's extension). Photos and credits: `research/grossglockner/photos/` (local only).

**Mood:** a crisp, clear October day before the road closes. High sun from the south, dry grass and grey rock, glaciers and snow peaks in the distance, and snow only where the photos show it. Ride it early, before the coaches and the motorbikes arrive at the Törl.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, road and roadside, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Dark asphalt, mid-grey in sun, smooth. Two lanes, two-way; the carriageway is 7.5 m since 1953 (WIKI-EN). Solid white edge line on the lower road; no centre line in any kept photo. Grey viaduct decks above the Törl with a light-grey metal railing, about 0.8 m high (est.). Dry-stone retaining walls with flat coping on the outer bends, 2 to 6 m high where visible. Wooden post-and-rail at the Törl apron and the viewpoint edge. No concrete barrier in any photo. Hairpins: 17 switchbacks from Ferleiten to the Hochtor, unnumbered (CYC-CC); no hairpin sign in any photo, so do not add one without a source. Snow poles: not visible in any kept photo.

**Ground and rock.** Rock grey and dark, schist-like at the Törl cut (P-06, P-08). Scree below the road (P-04, P-08). Bare rock from about 2040 m upward. Colours as hex: unknown; keep to the photo values, no pale mint or blue cast above the treeline. Glaciers visible from the Törl and the viaduct (P-01, P-03, P-05, P-09).

**Vegetation.** Spruce and larch forest from the valley to about 1750 m (species not verified). Treeline 1750 to 1800 m. Above it, dwarf shrub and grass to about 2080 m, then scree and bare rock. Alpine meadow with flowers from 3.8 to 22 km (P-04, P-09). Trees: none above the treeline. October colour of the larch: unknown.

**Buildings.** Fusch: church, hotels, tourist office, all in the valley. Ferleiten toll guardhouse (7.39). Gasthof Lukashansl (7.29). Oberstattgutalm restaurant (7.99, 182 m off). Wilfried-Haslauer-Haus (18.65). Edelweißhütte (20.0, 524 m off, outside the corridor). Römerhütte (22.1). F.A.T. Mankei (22.67). Architectural style, roofs and colours: unknown. Nothing tall above the road. Must never appear: concrete barriers. The route ends about 90 m into Carinthia at the Hochtor viewpoint; nothing beyond it is in scope.

**Landmarks.** Fuscher Törl memorial: square-plan stone pyramid, dark arched doorway, "SAXA TERRAE LOQUUNTUR GLORIAM TUAM" over the arch, two Austrian flags, stone-paved apron (P-02, read on the photo). Its history (road builders killed in weather accidents) comes from a search summary and is unverified [SRCH-MEM]. Fuscher Lacke, 2261 m (OSM, WIKI-DE). Hochtor viewpoint and history boards (OSM names: "Kelten und Römer am Glocknerweg", "Saumhandel über das Hochtor", "Menschenhandel über das Hochtor", "Die Glocknerroute in prähistorischer Zeit"). Hochtor summit 2576 m is above the route (WIKI-DE).

**Life.** Marmots: sourced [SRCH-ADAC; CYC-PZ], roadside positions unknown. Cyclists allowed, with the Ferleiten barrier; early morning has few cars (CYC-PZ). Cars and camper vans in the Törl car park (P-05). Motorbike riders parked at the Törl (P-05). Hikers on the apron (P-02, P-06). Coaches: a policy at the Törl forbade buses in 1995 [AF]; coaches seen elsewhere in photos are unverified. Work crews: unknown in October.

**Light.** Default: midday, clear sky, high sun from the south. Noon altitude about 35° (est., mid-October at 47.1 N); sunrise about 07:30 to 08:00 and sunset about 18:30 to 19:00 CEST (est.). Check the times with a sun calculator before use. Hero preset: the Törl viaduct and memorial in full south light with the glacier peaks behind; deep blue sky, no haze. Snow: patches only on north-facing slopes above about 2250 m, as in the late-summer photos (P-05, P-09). October snow: unknown, no October photo was found. Keep the road and the apron snow-free (P-08).

## Open issues

- **Pale, minty, "frosty" ground above the treeline.** The basemap.at orthophoto has a cool blue cast here. Reads wrong against the photos (P-04, P-09).
- **White patch at about 16 km** reads like snow. The photos show no snow there; re-colour it as scrub or grass.
- **Hillside at the Hochtor north portal** shows pale rippled faces. The photos show grey rock and scree.
- **Grass tufts read as blade spikes** at close range.
- **Dark near-black blue slopes in the lower valley at about 4 km.** Values are out of range for this scene.
- **Missing models:** snow poles (height 12 m is from an unverified search summary), hairpin signs, marmots, the Fuscher Törl memorial model.
- **Closure date:** late October [WIKI-DE]; early November appears in other summaries (unsourced). Use late October until checked.
- **Törl elevation differs by source** (2402 to 2428 m). The route file value 2428 m is used.
- **Start:** the route begins at Fusch (811 m); WIKI-DE uses Bruck (750 m). Whether to include the valley part is a scene decision.
- **Route gain** is unchanged at 1860 m: the extension through the tunnel is level.
- **Route gradients** in the file are sampling artefacts above 100 m; use 12 % as the maximum.
