# Cap de Formentor: art brief

The Ma-2210 from Port de Pollença to the Formentor lighthouse, Mallorca: 19.3 km from sea level to a high point
of about 220 m. The route has two climbs, the coll at the Mirador es Colomer and the ridge past El Fumat, with the
beach and pine forest in between. A turnaround loop at each end (322 m at the Port de Pollença start). Facts and
sources: `research/formentor/visual-reference.md` and `research/formentor/visual-qa-1.md` (43 dashcam matches).

**Mood:** dry, bright Mediterranean light on pale limestone and dark sea. A narrow road cut into the cliffs,
parapets of pale stone, umbrella pines, golden carritx grass, goats on the road near the cape. Late golden light
flatters it most; most reference frames are evening.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, road and roadside, light and atmosphere (sea).

## Look targets

**Road.** Mid-grey asphalt, smooth, few cracks, a dashed white centre line. The edge line is faint or absent on
most of the road; the centre line is continuous in the tunnel. Barriers follow the street-level frames:
- Pale grey-beige limestone block parapets, 0.8–1.0 m, with a flat coping, on the drop side of the coastal climbs.
- Galvanised W-beam over the sea on 13–14.5 km.
- Dry-stone walls in the forest section.
- Never Alpine posts or chevron forests.

Spanish signs: round no-overtaking and 40 limit, warning triangles, tunnel sign with a length plate, km posts.

**Cuts and rock.** Warm ochre to orange limestone (about #b88a55 to #d9a66a) in cuts 3–6 m high, with horizontal
bedding and a rubble apron at the foot, and carritx on top. Higher faces are grey limestone with karst texture.
Cliffs to the sea run 200–400 m. Wire rockfall nets on unstable banks. No overhanging walls where the real bank is
a grassy slope.

**Ground.** Dry tan-olive scrub (about #8c8a5e), golden carritx tussocks 0.8–1.5 m (about #b59a5a), and bare pale
limestone (about #b8b3a0). Red-brown soil and rubble on verges (about #6b5846 to #7a6a58). Never white "snow",
never bright sand strips beside the road.

**Vegetation.** Aleppo pines: tall, thin, often leaning or forked trunks, umbrella crowns in the top third,
grey-green. Holm oaks: dark, dense, wide. Dwarf fan palms of 1–2 m in groups of 3–6. Low understorey, gaps between
trunks. No hedges of round bushes, no lollipop trees, no grids of identical pines.

**Sea.** Deep cobalt offshore, turquoise over pale rock in the coves, sun glitter toward the sun, white-grey karst
coast. A true sea horizon, with Menorca on clear days in the east.

**Buildings.** Port de Pollença: plain plastered blocks of three to five floors, white or cream, with small
shutters and balconies. Hotel Formentor is a long low white building (1929). At the lighthouse: white masonry with
tile-capped walls and a squat café block. Nothing floating, everything on a terrace or cut.

**Life.** Goats on and beside the road near the cape, cyclists and hikers, sailboats and the beach ferry, the TIB
334 bus, very few cars past the beach (closed to cars 10:00–22:00 in season). Ravens and Eleonora's falcons over
the cliffs.

**Light.** Bright and warm with a high contrast between sunlit limestone and shade under the pines. Golden light
is the hero time.
