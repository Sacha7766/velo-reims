# Forêt de la Montagne de Reims loop: art brief

A 36.8 km loop over the Montagne de Reims from Rilly-la-Montagne (169 m), +498 m: west along the vines to
Villers-Allerand and Sermiers, up into the forest to Nanteuil-la-Forêt, across the plateau to Saint-Imoges and
Germaine, east to Ville-en-Selve, north over the Montagne on the D 33 to Ludes and back through Chigny-les-Roses. No
turnaround. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** The forest plateau of the Montagne de Reims at the end of August: long straight roads under beech and oak,
clearings with a village, then the vines and the plain on the way down. Signature view: the forest road across the
plateau to Saint-Imoges (km 12.6–19.6). Time preset: noon.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): vegetation, ground and terrain, light and atmosphere, sense of place.
