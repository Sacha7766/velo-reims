# Roizy and Suippe loop: art brief

A 35.2 km flat loop north of Reims from Bourgogne-Fresne (87 m), +96 m: north across the plain to Auménancourt and
Saint-Étienne-sur-Suippe in the Suippe valley (62 m), on to Poilcourt-Sydney, Houdilcourt and Sault-Saint-Remy, into
the Ardennes at Roizy (km 16.6, through its main street), then back south down the Suippe to Boult-sur-Suippe and
Bazancourt, over the plain to Pomacle and Fresne-lès-Reims (round the roundabout there, km 32.7) and back to
Bourgogne. Top 109 m at km 30.9. No turnaround. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** The open chalk plain and the Suippe valley at the end of August: stubble, beet and maize to the horizon,
the sugar works and silos at Bazancourt, poplars along the Suippe, small brick-and-chalk villages on the edge of the
Ardennes. Signature view: the Suippe valley from Saint-Étienne to Houdilcourt (km 6.9–12.9). Time preset: noon.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, road and roadside, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Plain roads as on the Plaine de Bétheny and Suippe loops (dashed white centre line, no edge lines, grass
to the asphalt, white posts with a red band). Widths unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Stubble, beet, maize and alfalfa (RPG 2025 parcels baked); the Suippe's meadows. Hex values:
unknown.

**Vegetation.** Few trees on the plain; poplars and willows along the Suippe. Species: unknown.

**Buildings.** Plain villages in render, chalk and brick, red-brown tiles; brick more common towards the Ardennes
(unknown in detail). The sugar works at Bazancourt: looks unknown.

**Landmarks.** The churches, mairies, memorials and Pomacle's water tower in the table (OSM); the Bazancourt sugar
works and silos (OSM landmarks baked from 4 km; the extract ends at 49.435° N, so landmarks north of Roizy are
missing).

**Life.** Tractors on the stubble; lorries near the sugar works (unknown).

**Light.** Default: noon.
