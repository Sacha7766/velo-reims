# Massif de Saint-Thierry loop: art brief

A 24.3 km loop north-west of Reims from Courcy (80 m) over the vineyard hills of the Massif de Saint-Thierry, +172 m
(baked profile; BRouter's raw sum is 262 m): Saint-Thierry, Merfy, Pouillon (the top, 145 m at km 8.0), Thil,
Villers-Franqueux, Hermonville and Cauroy-lès-Hermonville, then down to the canal past Loivre and back to Courcy. No
turnaround; about 3 km on the D 944 between Cauroy and Loivre (no paved minor road links them). The loop crosses each
village on its main road. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** late August on the slopes of a Champagne cru: vines in full leaf in straight rows down the hills, woods
on the crest, the plain and the cathedral city to the south-east. Signature view: the descent from Pouillon to Thil
through the vineyards (km 8–9). Time preset: golden.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, vegetation, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Rural roads as on the plain loops (research note: dashed white centre line, no edge lines, grass to the
asphalt, white posts with a red band); narrower in the vineyards (est. 4–5 m). The D 944: wider, edge lines unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Chalk under the vines, pale between the rows; stubble and beet on the plain parts as on the
Mont de Berru loop. Hex values: unknown.

**Vegetation.** Vineyards (OSM) in rows about 1.1 m apart down the slope, about 1.3 m high, in full leaf; woods on
the crest (Bois de Toussicourt, Bois de la Montagne). Species: unknown.

**Buildings.** As the plain villages: render, chalk and brick, red-brown tiles, slate on churches and mairies; wine
growers' houses and cellars (looks unknown).

**Landmarks.** The churches and memorials above (OSM), the monastery at Saint-Thierry, the water towers at
Saint-Thierry (26 m), Cauroy and Courcy (OSM).

**Life.** Unknown; vineyard tractors and pickers only from mid-September (harvest), so none in late August.

**Light.** Default: golden.
