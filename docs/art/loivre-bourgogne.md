# Loivre and Bourgogne loop: art brief

A 30.1 km flat loop north of Reims from Loivre (68 m) on the Aisne-Marne canal, +112 m: north to Berméricourt, east
along the D 30 across the plain to Bourgogne-Fresne, south through Fresne-lès-Reims and Bétheny, then west back to the
canal at Courcy and up the towpath valley to Loivre. No turnaround. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** The open chalk plain at the end of August: stubble and beet to the horizon, the odd water tower and silo,
the canal's poplars on the way back. Signature view: the D 30 across the plain to Bourgogne (km 2–7). Time preset:
noon.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, road and roadside, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Rural roads as on the Plaine de Bétheny loop; the D 30 straight across the plain. Widths unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Stubble, beet, maize and alfalfa (RPG 2025 parcels baked); chalk soil. Hex values: unknown.

**Vegetation.** Few trees: rows along the canal, copses on the plain. Species: unknown.

**Buildings.** Plain villages (render, chalk and brick, red-brown tiles); Bétheny suburban at its edge.

**Landmarks.** The churches, mairies, memorials and water towers in the table (OSM).

**Life.** Tractors on the stubble; traffic heavier near Bétheny.

**Light.** Default: noon.
