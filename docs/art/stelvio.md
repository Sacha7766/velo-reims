# Stelvio: art brief

The Passo dello Stelvio, east ramp, from Prad / Prato allo Stelvio (905 m, route km 0) up to the pass (2758 m in the route file). The route file says 25.32 km and the coordinates add up to the same; the research projection gives 25.26 km. The route ends on the pass plaza; the road continues to Umbrail and Bormio, outside the route file. Facts and sources: `research/stelvio/visual-reference.md`. Photos and credits: `research/stelvio/photos/manifest.json`.

**Mood:** a clear mid-October morning. Cold air at altitude, bright sun on the Ortler's snow faces, dry road. Traffic is lighter than in summer (est.). The signature view is the Tibet hut bend with the Ortler snow face behind it (P-4). Snow only on the Ortler and the glaciers (P-3, P-4, P-8); no snow patches on the road or verges, since the snow patches in the June photos are not an October look. Road status: open per the latest search result (dated 9 Oct, unverified). Verify with ANAS or Südtirol traffic info before use.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): road and roadside, ground and terrain, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Mid-grey asphalt with white edge lines [CM-kehre, CM-climb]. Surface condition "average" in 2015 [CYC-UP]. Two lanes, no shoulder, 5 to 6 m at the apex [GRAN, unverified]. Dry-stone walls on the uphill side of many bends (P-1, P-2). A tall stone parapet on the climb (P-2). Metal guard rail with timber posts on exposed outside bends near the valley (P-5); coverage elsewhere unknown. White and black pass sign with the Cima Coppi board at the top (P-9). Hairpin numbers on stones: not seen in any photo. Historic wooden avalanche tunnels, about 3.5 km on the east ramp, present state unknown (WIKI-DE).

**Ground and rock.** Pale grey bare limestone and scree above the treeline (P-10). Brown-red and grey stone in the walls (P-1). Geology per source: unknown. Hex values: unknown; keep to the photo greys. Snow patches on scree in June above about 2400 m; October snow: unknown. No white "snow" on the verges or the road.

**Vegetation.** Larch and spruce forest from Prad to about km 18 [PARK]; spruce dominant [PARK]; larch likely (est.). Pine grove at the valley edge (OSM). Treeline est. 2100 to 2200 m at km 17.5 to 18.7 [CYC-UP]. Above it, scree, alpine grass and bare rock. Zirbe (stone pine) above the forest in the eastern Alps is general, not Stelvio-specific [TREE]: unverified here. Larch colour in October: unknown.

**Buildings.** Prad: rendered valley-town houses, 2 to 3 storeys (OSM names; look est.). Gomagoi and Trafoi: South Tyrolean rendered villages, the Flachgau house style fits here (today's direction). Trafoi: white and grey-rendered houses (P-8); timber balconies: unknown. Franzenshoehe: white hotel blocks with hipped metal roofs and a stone-and-timber annex (P-7, old postcard). Pass top: Hotel Stilfserjoch, white render with dark-brown timber upper floor (P-3); timber shop facade with coloured shutters (P-9). Tibet hut: stone-and-timber round tower with conical metal roof (P-4). Must never appear: the generic large blocks now at the pass plaza, which do not match the pass hotels in P-3.

**Landmarks.** Cima Coppi sign reading 2760 m; the route file has 2758 m; the research uses 2757 m (P-9, WIKI-EN). Hotel Stilfserjoch (P-3). Tibet hut, 24.39 km, 204 m off the line [OSM, P-4]. Franzenshoehe Berghotel, 2188 m [WIKI-DE, P-7]. Ortler, 3905 m, south-east of the pass [WIKI-DE, P-3, P-4]. Kaiser-Franz-Joseph obelisk on the pass, 1925 [WIKI-DE]; not seen in any photo, position unknown. Trafoi memorial "Erstbesteigung des Ortler 1804" at 15.17 km [OSM name only].

**Life.** Road cyclists in groups, motorbikes stopped on the plaza, parked cars, souvenir shoppers [CM-top-hotels, CM-cima]. Peak day: more than 180 cars or bikes per hour (2015) [CYC-UP]; October lighter (est.). Hikers, animals and work crews: unknown.

**Light.** Default preset: midday, blue sky, cumulus at altitude (P-3, P-10); sun direction per hairpin unknown. Trafoi walls shade the lower road in the morning (est.). Hero preset: the Ortler snow face lit from the plaza (P-3) and the Tibet hut bend (P-4). Haze in the valley is flat (P-5). Sun angle for mid-October: unknown; check before use.
