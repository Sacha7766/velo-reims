# Petite Montagne loop: art brief

A 30.9 km loop south-west of Reims from Gueux (97 m), +186 m: south over the A 4 to Vrigny, then along the D 26 "route
du Champagne" through the vineyards of the Petite Montagne de Reims (Coulommes-la-Montagne, Pargny-lès-Reims,
Jouy-lès-Reims, Sacy, Écueil, Chamery), down across the plain by Villers-aux-Nœuds, Champfleury and Les Mesneux, and
back north through Ormes and Thillois. No turnaround. No road crosses the Petite Montagne's woods between Chamery and
Courmas, so the west side of the hill is the Ardre loop. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** The vineyard villages of the Petite Montagne in late August: the D 26 contouring the slope, vines above and
below, the woods on the crest, Reims and its cathedral across the plain. Signature view: Sacy to Chamery on the D 26
(km 8–11). Time preset: golden.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): vegetation, built environment, light and atmosphere, sense of place.

## Look targets

unknown.

**Vegetation.** Vineyards on the slope, woods on the crest (Bois d'Écueil, Bois de Chamery). Species: unknown.

**Buildings.** Wine villages: render and chalk, red-brown tiles, growers' houses with carriage gates (looks unknown).

**Landmarks.** The churches, mairies and memorials in the table (OSM).

**Life.** Unknown; no harvest yet at the end of August.
