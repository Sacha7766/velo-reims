# Alpe d'Huez: art brief

The D211 from Le Bourg-d'Oisans (718 m, route km 0) up the 21 numbered hairpins to the resort top, Avenue du Rif Nel (1823 m). The route file says 14.9 km; the coordinates add up to 14.70 km; the official climb is 13.7 to 13.8 km. The route ends at Rif Nel, with a turnaround on a real OSM loop at the resort top (open issue 7). Facts and sources: `research/alpe-dhuez/visual-reference.md`. Photos and credits: `research/alpe-dhuez/photos/manifest.json`.

**Mood:** a clear mid-October morning. Dry road, cool air, sun from the south over the resort. Snow only on the distant peaks (P-3, P-6). No snow on roofs or road, because the winter dusk photo (P-4) is not a mid-October look. The signature view is the upper resort looking down the Oisans valley toward the snow peaks (P-3, km 13.5). Traffic density is unknown; keep it light.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): road and roadside, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Asphalt colour unknown; well surfaced [BIKE-MAG], cracks on the final bends [SRCH]. Hairpins are wide and flat [SRCH]. Painted winners' names across the lanes [BIKE-MAG, P-1]; "Alpe * Huez" letters on the grass slope (P-1); white centre dashes on the lower lane (P-1). Concrete wall on the uphill side in places, graffiti on the faces [BIKE-MAG]; retaining walls mapped at 4.07 km and 8.9 to 10.7 km [OSM]. Other barriers, road width and posts: unknown. Hairpin signs: 21 numbered, one Tour winner each [PCUK, WIKI-EN]; their look and altitude values: unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Grey limestone cliffs and scree above the road: 5.8 to 6.7 km, 9.0 to 9.7 km, 12.2 to 13.9 km [OSM], photos P-2 and P-7. Hex values: unknown; keep to the photo greys. Grass banks at 2 to 6 km (P-1). Cut heights: unknown.

Tree line est. 1300 to 1500 m. Season colour: unknown. Dense conifers at 7.5 km are not in the research (section 5 lists cliffs, scree and meadow for 6 to 9 km).

**Buildings.** Savoie ski-resort style for the resort and Huez: white or beige render and concrete, grey roofs, timber upper storeys, plain timber balconies, brown shutters, no geraniums (today's house style set). Bourg-d'Oisans: 2 to 4 storeys, rendered and tiled [OSM]. La Garde: small village with a tower church, roofs unknown. Must never appear: a lone hotel block on the slope at 8.9 km (no source places one).

**Life.** Cyclists: strong presence in summer [SRCH]; no count. Cars open outside Tour days; RD211 closed to vehicles 23 to 26 July 2026 [SRCH-CLOSE]; traffic density unknown. Cable car gondolas and pylons above the lower road (P-1, P-3). Cows and paragliders: unknown.
