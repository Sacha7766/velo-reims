# Wolfgangsee: art brief

The lake road from the St. Gilgen centre (route km 0, 549 m) along the B158 through Strobl, then St. Wolfgang Straße on the east shore to the St. Wolfgang Marktplatz (route end, km 18.20, 552 m). Length 18.20 km projected (the file says 18.31), gain 104 m, elevation 539.5–567.3 m in the route file. The route ends in the Marktplatz, a living street; the turnaround is a real ~100 m loop through the old town, joined flush to the road. Facts and sources: `research/wolfgangsee/visual-reference.md`. Photos and credits: `research/wolfgangsee/photos/manifest.json`.

**Mood:** a clear mid-October morning on the lake. Low sun from the east over the water, lake mist that lifts (est.), orange and yellow trees on the far shore, snow on the highest peaks (C-4). Signature view: St. Wolfgang's white church with its grey spire over the lake, seen from the east shore at km 16–18. Time preset: see Light.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): Sense of place, Built environment, Light and atmosphere.

## Look targets

**Road.** B158 Wolfgangseestraße (primary) from km 6.95 to about 9.8, then through Strobl. Asphalt, two lanes at km 7.1–7.5, three at 9.5 (OSM). St. Wolfgang Straße (secondary) from km 12.82. Cycle path beside the B158 for most of the route (RAD); its km: unknown. Width, centre lines, edge lines, kerbs and rails: unknown. Speed limit 50 km/h is from a search snippet, unverified. No tunnels. Bridges at km 0.63, 5.13, 5.55, 6.03, 6.49, 6.61, 10.24, 12.85, 13.83, 14.38, 15.08; culverts at 8.7, 9.6, 14.0, 14.4, 16.9 (OSM). The Marktplatz is a living street with a footway passage at 18.20 (OSM).

**Ground and rock.** Lakeside ground: planted lawns in St. Gilgen (OSM, km 0–1); open lakeside meadows at km 2–4 (OSM). Schafberg: bare rock summit and cliffs (C-5, C-8, direction est.). Rock type and colours as hex: unknown. Shore: timber jetties at the water (C-1, C-7); no shore-line detail in any source.

**Vegetation.** Species: unknown in all sources. Spruce-dominated slopes in the photos (C-5, C-8, C-9); beech est. on the lower slopes and lake edge. Autumn colour: orange and yellow deciduous trees in St. Gilgen (C-4) and on the left shore (C-9, a 1993 snapshot). Peak colour date: unknown. Density by km from OSM: trees 8–14 within 100 m at km 8–9 and 12–13, few at 2–4 and 4–6.

**Buildings.** St. Gilgen: cream villas, red and grey roofs, onion-dome spire (C-3, C-4, C-7); hotels Stern, Kendler, Brunetti, Radetzky within 90 m of the start (OSM). Strobl: summer villas from about 1900, guesthouses along km 11.9–12.3 (STROBL, OSM). St. Wolfgang: white or cream plaster, dark or red-tile pitched roofs, 3–4 storey lakefront hotels with red awnings and terraces, boathouses at the shore (C-5, C-6). Balconies, flower boxes and green shutters appear in single hotel captions only (low confidence, not a village rule). Old-town street widths: unknown.
**Landmarks.** The lake: 12.4 km², 10.3 km long, about 2 km wide, 114 m deep (SAL-LAKE); the lake outline is OSM relation 1764080 (LAKE), partly contradicted at km 4.5–15. St. Wolfgang parish church: white plaster, grey pointed spire, clock face, km 18.20 (C-5, C-6, OSM). Schafberg: about 1782 m, cog railway runs to 1 Nov 2026 (SCHAF, WIKI-OLD). Zwölferhorn: 1521 m (WIKI-OLD), south of St. Gilgen. Bürglstein wooded hill north of Strobl, right of the route at km 13–15 (STROBL, est.). Platonic Lovers bronze in Strobl (STROBL). Seeklause kayak slipway, km 12.17 (STROBL).

**Life.** Passenger ship ÖSTERREICH on the open lake (C-2); fleet of 5 or 6 is disputed. Dinghies and moored sailboats in the bays (C-1, C-3, C-7). Cyclists on the B158 cycle path (RAD). Cars on the B158, heavy in summer (RAD). Paragliders: the Zwölferhorn landing field is about 400 m west of the start (OSM); paragliders over the lake are est. only. Swans and ducks: unknown. A small rowing boat or kayak in C-6.

**Light.** Default preset: sunrise 07:19 CEST and sunset 18:26 CEST on 12 October (SUN, est. ±3 min). Sun direction (est.): low from the ESE in the morning, WSW in the afternoon, since the route runs NW to SE. Hero preset: the morning ride, low sun over the water, lake mist (est.), clear sky with snow on the peaks (C-4). Water temperature in October: unknown (summer 22–26 C, HYDRO; est. 15–18 C in October).

## Don't touch

- Houses whose footprint reached the asphalt in St. Wolfgang are removed. The road stays clear of house corners.
- The turnaround: the ~100 m loop through the old town joins the road flush.
- The Marktplatz as the route end.
- No lake-level shoulder at km 9.5–15 until the shore is checked.
