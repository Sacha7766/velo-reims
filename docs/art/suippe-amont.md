# Haute Suippe loop: art brief

A 34.7 km loop east of Reims from Warmeriville (81 m), +136 m (baked profile; BRouter's raw sum is 222 m): over the
plateau to Ménil-Lépinois and Aussonce, past wind turbines to La Neuville-en-Tourne-à-Fuy (the top, about 142 m at km
15), down to Bétheniville, then back down the Suippe valley through Pontfaverger-Moronvilliers, Selles, Saint-Masmes
and Heutrégiville. No turnaround, no road ridden twice; each village is crossed on its main road. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** late August: the open chalk plateau after the harvest under a big sky, then the green river valley with
poplars and water meadows. Signature view: the wind turbines beside the road at km 17. Time preset: golden.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, vegetation, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

posts with a red band) [regional]. Valley road through the villages: unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Plateau: stubble, beet and bare chalk as on the plain loops. Valley: meadows by the river.

**Vegetation.** Plateau: few trees, copses. Valley: trees along the Suippe (species unknown; poplars expected but

**Landmarks.** Wind turbines (OSM, 150 m), the water tower at Bétheniville, the chimneys at Pontfaverger and
Heutrégiville, the mast at km 26.3, the château at Heutrégiville (OSM).
