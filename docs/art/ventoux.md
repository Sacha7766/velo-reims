# Mont Ventoux: art brief

The Mont Ventoux climb from Bédoin (309 m, route km 0) up the south side to the summit at the route end (1900 m, km 21.19 projected). Gain 1589 m in the route file, 7.4 % average, steepest 500 m window 10.9 % at km 8.0–8.5. The summit end is a synthetic turning circle, so the rider turns back there. Facts and sources: `research/ventoux/visual-reference.md` (its route length 21.19 km is the projection; the file says 21.41). Photos and credits: `research/ventoux/photos/manifest.json`.

**Mood:** a clear mid-October morning on the south side. Dry grey asphalt, bright blue sky, pale limestone scree catching the sun above the trees, cool air (summit mean max 9.9 C, min 5.2 C in October). The wind is the risk: mistral on 151 days a year at the summit. Signature view: the bare scree above Chalet Reynard with the summit transmitter on the skyline ahead. Time preset: see Light.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): Ground and terrain, Road and roadside, Sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Grey asphalt with dark patched cracks, two lanes. White edge lines and a dashed centre line on the upper road (PH-5, PH-8). Centre stripe on the lower road and road width: unknown. Yellow-topped white D974 milestones with gradient and distance to the summit, with a stone kerb and rubble below (PH-5). Concrete parapet kerb at the Chalet Reynard car park (PH-4). Red-and-white guide posts on the forest edge and red snow posts at km 13.8 (PH-8). OSM: one guard rail in the 16.0–17.5 bin; wall barriers, about 14 features, at km 18.5–21.5. Hairpin count and painted road names: unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Upper slopes (km 15–21): white to pale grey limestone, loose angular stones from pebble to fist size, sharp-edged, not river-rounded. Near white in sun, grey-blue in shade (PH-7). Summit: bare calcareous rock, no trees (WIKI-EN). Hex values and cut heights: unknown. Plants on the scree: sparse dry grass tufts and low shrubs, about 5–10 % cover (PH-1, PH-2, PH-7, est.). Above km 15 the ground is bare scree with a few tufts, not a grass lawn.

**Vegetation.** Biome for plants: Alpine, as set on 2026-10-12. The Mediterranean species in the source altitude table (Aleppo pine, holm oak, garrigue lavender) are not used for the plant set; palms never. Source bands: black pine 1480–1650 m and beech 1130–1660 m (WIKI-FR); junipers just above the forest (WIKI-EN); dwarf pines below km 17 (PH-3, OSM needle forest at 16.2). Cedar stand on the Bédoin side at 800–1000 m (SRCH-VEG). Forest on the line: pines and bare broadleaf trees, about km 5 to 14.9 (est.). Crown shapes, understorey and October colour: unknown.

**Buildings.** Houses use the provence style in the scene config: warm stone and ochre render, faded canal-tile roofs, blue-grey and sage shutters. Bédoin is a stone Provence village by look (est.; no source gives its style). Chalet Reynard: white two-storey, brown roof edge (PH-3). Summit: bare concrete industrial blocks; transmitter a concrete shaft with a red-and-white antenna on a plain concrete base block. Shaft height est. 50 m (sources 40–69 m). Never: palms, or a Mediterranean biome.

**Landmarks.** Bédoin start sign "LE MONT VENTOUX 1909 m" with the Simpson plaque, km 0 (PH-6). Saint-Estève, km 5.47, 542 m (OSM). Chalet Reynard, km 14.95, 1418 m (OSM, ROUTE, PH-3). D974 milestone, est. km 15.2 (PH-5). Tom Simpson memorial, km 19.84, 1772 m (OSM, PH-1, PH-2, MEM-1). Summit transmitter (OSM tag 78 m; WIKI-EN 50 m; WIKI-FR over 40 m; SRCH-SUM 60 m and 69 m). Chapelle Sainte-Croix, km 20.97, 1884 m, 15th-century (OSM, WIKI-EN). Summit: 1909 m on the sign (PH-6), 1910 m on Wikipedia, route file ends at 1900 m.

**Life.** Cars: many on the Bédoin side; parked cars at Chalet Reynard and the summit car park (PH-3, PH-4, PH-8). Cyclists on the climb (PH-5, PH-6, PH-8). Motorbikes at the summit car park (PH-4). Sheep near the pines above Chalet Reynard (Commons search, not in the manifest). Hikers at Chalet Reynard and on the crest (SRCH-ROAD). Paragliders: unknown.

**Light.** Default time preset: unknown; no source gives sun time or direction for Ventoux. Hero preset: clear blue sky, strong sun on pale scree (PH-1, PH-3, PH-7), hard shadows from the pines at km 13.8 (PH-8). Wind: mistral on 151 days a year at the summit (WIKI-FR); 90 km/h winds on about 240 days a year (WIKI-EN); closures are wind-driven, no October schedule found. Record gusts 313 km/h and 320 km/h (WIKI-FR).
