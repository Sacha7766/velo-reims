# Sa Calobra: art brief

The Ma-2141 from the port of Sa Calobra on the Serra de Tramuntana coast up to the top of the route near Coll dels Reis, Mallorca: 9.69 km, 13.5 m at the port to 673.8 m at the route end (the pass is 682 m, see Open issues). A single-lane road, built 1928–1933 by Antonio Parietti, with a 270° loop (the Nus de sa Corbata, the tie knot) about 0.5 km short of the top. Facts and sources: `research/sa-calobra/visual-reference.md`. Photos and credits: `research/sa-calobra/photos/` (local only).

**Mood:** a clear late-spring or autumn morning before the coaches. Pale limestone walls, the road hanging above a deep blue sea, dry grey rock and scrub, and the knot's stone arch in high sun. Riders early, before 09:00, when the road is still quiet.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): road and roadside, ground and terrain, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Single-lane asphalt, mid-grey (P-03 sunlit lighter), smooth, few cracks. No hard shoulder on most of the climb. A solid white edge line where the road has one (on the lower road of the knot and the upper curves). Centre line: unknown; none in the knot photos. Stone walls with dressed coping on the drop side of the knot and the 4–6 km section. Galvanised W-beam or painted metal rail on the outer edge of the knot loops and above the 2.6 km tunnel. Passing places exist but their positions are unknown, so do not invent them. Metal km or delineator posts: white with a red band (seen at the left edge of P-01 and the lower road of P-02; not confirmed as km markers). No hairpin chevron boards are visible in the photos: do not add them without a source.

**Ground and rock.** Pale grey-cream limestone, bare with scattered low scrub in the cracks and at the foot of walls. Build the knot's rock in cool grey-white (est. #c8c4b8 to #b6b2a6) with darker shadow in the joints. Cliffs of grey limestone at 2.2–2.4 km and 9.0–9.5 km. Rubble and dry-stone walls: local limestone, coursed or random, cream to grey, dressed coping on top (est. 0.3 m wide).

**Vegetation.** Sparse. Low dark-green rounded scrub in rock crevices and by the walls; tufts of tall dry grass (carritx-like, unconfirmed) on the lower slopes and rubble under the knot. No forest on the route. Trees: only at the port (unknown species). Do not add pines or oaks on the climb: no source or photo shows them.

**Buildings.** The port hamlet: plastered houses, cream and white render, pitched roofs with tiles, a 4-storey hotel (antic hotel Sa Calobra) and small restaurant buildings. Refugi: one storey. At the knot: a timber-roofed shelter with slatted sloping roof and a stone-walled viewpoint terrace (P-02). The Coll dels Reis bar: one-storey terrace building (est. 10 m × 6 m, unverified). Nothing tall above the road.

**Landmarks.** The knot (route 9.465–9.612 km), the Nus de sa Corbata: 270° loop, single-arch masonry bridge, parapets with dressed caps, "blends in colour with the surrounding rock" [MALL-COM]. Torrent de Pareis viewpoint and beach (39.85226, 2.80572), a gorge off the road, not on the route. Coll dels Reis saddle (39.82754, 2.81799, 682 m) at the top. The Torre d'Es Bosc defensive tower at the port (39.8508, 2.80216).

**Life.** Cyclists: many, mostly on weekends and in spring; advised early morning. Coaches: take turns to pass in the single lane, and fill the road on the descent. Cars: parked at the knot viewpoint (3–5 cars in P-02 and P-03). Walkers at the viewpoint. Goats, motorbikes and birds: unknown on this road in the sources.

**Light.** Default: high morning sun, clear blue sky with a few cirrus, deep blue sea to both sides of the knot. Hero preset: the same clear sky, sun on the pale walls, high contrast on the arch shadow. Evening (P-02) is warm and low, which flatters the stone; use it for a second preset only.

## Don't touch

Nothing is built yet for this route. When the first build exists, the knot's over-pass and under-pass geometry (the arc, the arch and the crossing) is the first thing to protect, and the sea position (left of the climb going up).

## Open issues

- **Route ends about 0.5 km short of the pass.** The route file ends at the knot's turnaround (39.83175, 2.81630, 673.8 m). OSM continues the Ma-2141 from there (way 1306219884) for about 0.5 km to the saddle node (39.82754, 2.81799, 682 m). Decide whether the route should be extended before the knot is built.
- **Hairpin count is disputed** (14 between km 6.1 and 9.6 by PJAMM; 26 by other sources; "several dozen" by Click-Mallorca). Do not use a number in the HUD or the brief.
- **Knot dimensions unknown.** The arch opening (est. 4–6 m wide, 3–4 m high) and parapet heights (est. 0.9–1.1 m) are eyeballed from the photos. Measure a real survey if one becomes available.
- **Tunnel**: OSM tags a 28 m road tunnel at 2.62 km. Sources only mention tunnels on the beach path. Check street-level before building the portal.
- **Road width** is unknown (single lane). A 2 m figure from a rider's account is unverified.
- **Cyclists at the top are a guess from the photos.** Good for the scene, but do not claim a number.
- **Mirrors, km markers, hairpin signs**: unknown from the sources. Do not add them without a photo.

## Blender build list (the five most iconic objects)

These five objects are what make the road recognisable. Dimensions are est. unless a source gives them.

1. **The knot's single-arch masonry bridge and upper parapet.** route 9.465 km (under-pass), 9.612 km (over-pass); lon 2.81589, lat 39.83196 for the under-pass. Arch est. 4–6 m wide, 3–4 m high; deck est. 7 m wide; parapet est. 1.0 m high with a dressed cap. Materials: pale limestone ashlar in the arch, random rubble in the walls. Colours: cream-grey, est. #c8c0ae, with darker joints.
2. **The stone retaining wall of the 4–6 km section.** route 4.0–6.0 km (for example 5.0 km at lon 2.80426, lat 39.83485). Height est. 4–8 m on the cut side and est. 1.0 m parapet at the top. Materials: coursed or random rubble limestone, cream to grey, dressed coping about 0.3 m wide. Wall=dry_stone at 9.06 km for a shorter section.
3. **The short tunnel portal on the Ma-2141.** route 2.626–2.654 km (lon 2.79881, lat 39.84296 to lon 2.79878, lat 39.84324). Length est. 28 m (OSM). Portal est. 6 m wide, plain pale stone or concrete, no lights confirmed. Materials: rock cut with a plain portal. Colour: cream-grey.
4. **The viewpoint shelter and car park at the knot.** route 9.525 km (lon 2.81630, lat 39.83213). Timber-roofed shelter est. 6 m × 3 m with a sloping slatted roof, a "P" sign with a WC pictogram, and a stone-walled car park on the loop. Colours: honey timber, grey stone.
5. **The Coll dels Reis bar terrace.** route 9.654 km (lon 2.81619, lat 39.83195). One storey, terrace with parasols, est. 10 m × 6 m, white render (unverified). Sits beside the knot's upper loop.
