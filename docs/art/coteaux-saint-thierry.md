# Coteaux de Saint-Thierry loop: art brief

A 33.2 km loop over the hills north-west of Reims from Saint-Thierry (138 m), +468 m: down through Merfy to Macô and
Châlons-sur-Vesle at the foot of the massif (82 m), up past Chenay to Trigny, over the ridge to Prouilly and Pévy,
north to Bouvancourt, then east along the D 30 through Hermonville, by Villers-Franqueux and up to Pouillon (144 m)
and Thil. Top 208 m at km 23.7, on the plateau between Bouvancourt and Hermonville. No turnaround. It shares only
Saint-Thierry, Merfy, Pouillon and Thil with the Massif de Saint-Thierry loop, ridden the other way round. Research
notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** Late August on the Massif de Saint-Thierry: vines on the south and east slopes, woods on the crests, stubble
and beet on the plateau, the plain and Reims below to the south-east. Signature view: the climb from Prouilly to Pévy
and on to the plateau (km 14–17). Time preset: golden.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, vegetation, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Rural roads as on the other Reims loops (dashed white centre line, no edge lines, grass to the asphalt,
white posts with a red band); the D 30 east of Bouvancourt a little wider. Widths unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Chalk under the vines, pale between the rows; stubble, beet and maize on the plateau (RPG 2025
parcels baked). Hex values: unknown.

**Vegetation.** Vineyards (OSM, RPG) in rows down the slopes in full leaf; woods on the crests of the massif. Species:
unknown.

**Buildings.** Champagne villages as on the plain: render, chalk and brick, red-brown tiles, slate on churches and
mairies; growers' houses and cellars (looks unknown).

**Landmarks.** The churches, mairies and memorials in the table (OSM); the château at Merfy.

**Life.** Unknown; no harvest yet at the end of August.

**Light.** Default: golden.
