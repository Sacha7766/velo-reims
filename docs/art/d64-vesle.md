# D 64 and Vesle loop: art brief

A 41.3 km flat loop east and south-east of Reims from Cernay-lès-Reims (133 m), +204 m: south 2 km on the D 364, then
the D 64 under the south side of the Mont de Berru to Nogent-l'Abbesse, east to Beine-Nauroy, south across the plain
past the Reims-Champagne aerodrome to Prunay, round by Val-de-Vesle to Beaumont-sur-Vesle, north-west along the Vesle
to Sillery, Taissy and Saint-Léonard and back north to Cernay. Cernay's first 2 km are ridden out and back (the D 64
leaves the D 364 there). No turnaround. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** The plain south of the Mont de Berru at the end of August: the mount's wooded slope and the TDF mast above,
open stubble, the Vesle valley's poplars and the canal. Signature view: the D 64 under the Mont de Berru (km 2–6).
Time preset: noon.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, road and roadside, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Plain roads as on the Mont de Berru loop; the D 64 a two-lane road along the foot of the mount. Widths
unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Stubble, beet, maize and alfalfa (RPG 2025 parcels baked); the mount's wooded slope.

**Vegetation.** Few trees on the plain; poplars and willows along the Vesle and the canal; woods on the Mont de Berru.

**Buildings.** Plain villages; Taissy and Saint-Léonard suburban at their edge.

**Landmarks.** The TDF mast on the Mont de Berru (seen from the D 64), the churches, mairies and memorials in the
table (OSM).

**Life.** Tractors on the stubble; traffic heavier near Reims.

**Light.** Default: noon.
