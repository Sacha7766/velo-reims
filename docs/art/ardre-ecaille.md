# Ardre and L'Écaille loop: art brief

A 28.7 km loop west of Reims from Gueux (97 m), +363 m: north to Muizon on the Vesle, west to Rosnay, south past
Janvry and the lieu-dit L'Écaille, over to Méry-Prémecy in the Ardre valley, east through Saint-Euphraise, Bouilly and
Courmas below the Petite Montagne, and back north by Jouy, Pargny, Coulommes-la-Montagne and Vrigny. No turnaround.
Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** The quieter west side of the Petite Montagne at the end of August: small valley villages, vines on the
slopes, woods on the hill tops. Signature view: the climb from Janvry to the ridge above Méry (km 7.7–10.4). Time
preset: golden.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, vegetation, built environment, sense of place.
