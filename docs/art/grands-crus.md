# Grands Crus loop: art brief

A 32.8 km loop on the north flank of the Montagne de Reims from Ludes (145 m), +419 m: east along the vines to
Mailly-Champagne, down to the plain at Puisieulx and Sillery (87 m), back up through the grand cru vineyards to
Verzenay and its lighthouse, by Verzy and up through the forest past Mont Sinaï and the Chapelle Saint-Basle (top 286
m at km 17.8), down to Louvois, west to Ville-en-Selve and back north over the Montagne on the D 33 to Ludes. Sacha
asked for the D 23 between Ludes and Ville-en-Selve; OSM tags that road D 33. One sharp corner at Louvois (km 23.7),
no turnaround. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md) (regional).

**Mood:** The grands crus at the end of August: vines from the plain up to the forest edge, the Phare de Verzenay
among them, then the beech woods of the Montagne. Signature view: Verzenay and its lighthouse over the vines (km
14.2–14.7). Time preset: golden.

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): vegetation, ground and terrain, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Vineyard roads (D 26) and forest roads (D 34, D 33); the climb through the forest narrower. Widths unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Chalk under the vines; the forest floor on the Montagne (clay and millstone, unknown).

**Vegetation.** Vineyards on the slope up to the forest edge; beech and oak forest on top (the Faux de Verzy, twisted
beeches, lie near the D 34 above Verzy; whether the road passes them is unknown).

**Buildings.** Grand cru villages: render and chalk, red-brown tiles, champagne houses' cellars and gates (looks
unknown).

**Landmarks.** The Phare de Verzenay (OSM, 97 m left of km 14.7), the Mont Sinaï observatory (141 m right of km 16.9),
the Chapelle Saint-Basle (13 m left of km 17.3), the churches and mairies in the table.

**Life.** Unknown; no harvest yet at the end of August.

**Light.** Default: golden.
