# Mont de Berru loop: art brief

A 31.4 km loop on the Champagne plain north-east of Reims, from Witry-lès-Reims (137 m) through Caurel, Lavannes,
Époye, Beine-Nauroy, Nogent-l'Abbesse, Berru and Cernay-lès-Reims, +245 m. The one hill of the area is the Mont de
Berru: the route climbs to Nogent-l'Abbesse (182 m) and tops out at 200 m on its flank at km 25.9, between Berru and
Cernay. No turnaround. The loop crosses each village on its main road, not via the mairie. Research notes:
[research/champagne/visual-reference.md](../../research/champagne/visual-reference.md).

**Mood:** a late-August afternoon after the cereal harvest: pale chalky stubble to the horizon, dark green sugar beet,
a high pale-blue sky with thin cloud, warm dry air. Signature view: the plain opening below the Mont de Berru flank
at km 26. Time preset: golden (unknown until Sacha picks a season).

**Hero categories** (scorecard, need 4+): ground and terrain, road and roadside, built environment, sense of place.

## Look targets

**Road.** Rural roads: two lanes, est. 5–6 m, mid-grey asphalt with lighter patches, white dashed centre line with
long gaps, no edge lines, no shoulder, grass verge to the asphalt. White delineator posts with a red band. Village
streets: curbs, pavements, painted bays, raised crossings, tall street lamps, overhead wires on poles. Roundabouts:
white edge line, blue round sign. Km markers and village signs: unknown.

**Ground and rock.** Late August: stubble and bare soil pale beige to light grey-brown with chalk showing, sugar beet
dark green and low, maize tall and mid green, verges green with dry tall grass at the field edge. Chalky white field
tracks. Hex values: unknown until sampled.

**Vegetation.** Few trees in the plain; copses as dark islands; rows of round-crowned broadleaf trees at some
junctions; woods on the Mont de Berru (OSM). Species: unknown. No spruce.

**Buildings.** Two-storey houses, cream or beige render or pale stone, red-brown tile roofs with a low pitch, some
dark grey roofs, red brick gate pillars, white garage doors, workshops with sawtooth roofs. Never: Alpine timber
balconies, log stacks, Salzburg farmhouses.

**Landmarks.** The churches of each village and the war memorials (OSM positions, looks unknown until photos),
the observatory tower at Beine-Nauroy, the masts at km 16.7, 27.5 and 28.4 (18–24 m, OSM).

**Life.** Cars on the rural roads (PNX-4, PNX-7). Tractors, cyclists, animals: unknown.

**Light.** Default: golden. Hero: unknown until a season is chosen.
