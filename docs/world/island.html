Serpentine Island

All fourteen routes on one invented island, joined by new roads, so you can ride from the Mallorca coast over the Alps to the city without a loading screen, like Los Santos in GTA or Zwift's Watopia. Each route keeps its real road, heights, aerial photos, buildings and trees. The land and roads between them are made up to fit.

  • 14real routes
  • 254 kmreal road
  • ≈ 150 kmnew road, 17 links
  • 75 × 60 kma bit smaller than Mallorca
  • 2,758 mhighest road, Stelvio

How it fits together

  • Real heights stay real. Every summit keeps its true height, so the island is high in the middle and low at the coast: Mallorca and Sydney sit on the sea, the lakes at 550 m, the alpine villages at 720–900 m and the passes above them.
  • North stays up. Routes are moved but never turned, so the light in their aerial photos matches the sun and every hairpin stays where it is.
  • Each route brings its surroundings. About 1.5 km of real ground either side of the road, a lake or a coast where it has one. Beyond that a soft seam blends into invented land.
  • New roads are short and rideable. Mostly 5 to 11 km, never steeper than about 6.5 % on average, with hairpins where the height has to be climbed.
  • Real junctions where they exist. The Sault road joins the Ventoux at Chalet Reynard as it does in France; Strobl and Spitz are real places on their routes.
  • Summits that end in reality end here too. Gaisberg, Kitzbüheler Horn, Ventoux, the Formentor lighthouse and the port of Sa Calobra are turnarounds.

Loops to ride

  • The two giants 77 km · about 2,200 m climbing
    Großglockner up, over the High road, down the Stelvio, back along the valley.
  • Danube and the Alpe 78 km · about 1,700 m climbing
    Prato down to the Danube, the Wachau to Melk, the gorge to Bourg, the 21 hairpins, the Col road back.
  • Tramuntana and the lakes 48 km · about 800 m climbing
    Up from Port de Pollença to the Coll dels Reis, over to the Wolfgangsee, round to Strobl, past the Gaisberg and down to the sea.
  • Three cities 43 km · about 450 m climbing
    The Ring, Central Park and Bondi joined by new city streets.

Plus the out-and-back climbs from each loop, and everything at once: about 400 km of road.

Riding it

  • Junctions. The HUD shows the turn ahead; the Zwift Ride's D-pad or the arrow keys pick it. Without a choice you stay on your road.
  • World map. See where you are and start anywhere on the island.
  • Named routes stay. "Ride the Stelvio" starts you in Prato, with the same banners, records, ghosts and segments as today.
  • GPS and Strava. The island has no real place on Earth, so rides get coordinates on an empty patch of ocean, as Zwift does with Watopia, and show as a Virtual Ride. Real Strava segments can't match there, but the app still knows the real road under you and can time those segments itself. The single routes stay as they are, with real GPS, for anyone who wants real segments on Strava.

Build order

  1. Spike: two routes and one road. Gaisberg and Wolfgangsee joined by the 5.5 km Strobl road. Proves the land builder (real ground inside, filled land outside, the seam), one new road, a junction and streaming, measured on the M5 Max and an older MacBook Air. 1–2 weeks
  2. The hard seam. Add Cap de Formentor over the Down to the sea road: sea level to mountains, Mallorca photos next to Austrian ones, two kinds of trees and ground blending. about 1 week
  3. The High Alps as the first world you can ride. Großglockner, Stelvio, Kitzbüheler Horn and Alpe d'Huez with the valley roads, the High road and the Col road: about 77 km real and 40 km new. World map, rides on the road network, FIT files with island coordinates. 3–5 weeks
  4. Coast, lakes, Danube and Ventoux. Region by region, each shipped when it looks right. a few weeks
  5. The City. Last, because invented streets and buildings between three real cities are the most art work.
  6. Ride together on the island. The 1.3 plan, but everyone in one world instead of on one road.

All in all a few months. Each step ships on its own, and today's routes keep working the whole time. The engine work from 1.1 (ride time from the clock, freeing memory after a ride) comes first, because streaming a world needs both.

For you to decide

  • The layout. Mallorca north, Alps in the middle, Provence southwest, the city east. Move anything you want somewhere else.
  • The name. "Serpentine Island" is a placeholder.
  • The cities. On the island, last (my suggestion), or a separate city world.
  • Real heights. Keep them (my suggestion) or level the routes to fit tighter, which would cost the summits their real numbers.
  • Today's single routes. Keep them as a mode with real GPS (my suggestion).
