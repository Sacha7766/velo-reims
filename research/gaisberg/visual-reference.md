# Gaisberg (Salzburg) — visual reference for the 3D scene

Scope: facts for the Gaisbergstraße (L108 "Gaisberg Höhenstraße") climb and summit. Photos in `photos/` (44 files, manifest in `photos/manifest.json`). "unknown" = not found in any source I could open.

Conventions: `[src]` = a source URL I fetched or saw in search results. Items marked *(photo)* are visible in a file in `photos/` that I looked at. Items marked *(search summary)* came from a search result and were not opened.

## Discrepancies to resolve before building

- Road length: brief says 10 km. Austria-Forum says the Gaisberg road is 8.6 km [src: https://austria-forum.org/af/AustriaWiki/Gaisberg_%28Salzburg%29]. Quäldich lists four ascents: Elsbethen 10.7 km, Guggenthal 8.8 km, Aigen 8.2 km, Wiestal 13.4 km [src: https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/gaisberg/]. The 10 km figure probably corresponds to the Elsbethen or Guggenthal approach, not the L108 proper. Unknown which exact route the brief means.
- Summit elevation: 1287 m [src: austria-forum above]; 1288 m [src: quaeldich, "Beschreibung"]; 1265 m [src: quaeldich, heading, probably the Gaisbergspitze car-park level]; 1286 m [search summary, salzburg24]. Use 1287 m as the anchor unless a survey says otherwise.
- Summit GPS: quaeldich gives 47.8033 N, 13.1109 E [src: https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/gaisberg/]. Brief gives 47.8038 N, 13.1110 E. Difference ~0.5 km north. Unknown which is correct for the summit node.
- Guggenthal start: quaeldich gives the B158 junction at 665 m; brief says ~570 m. Unknown which is correct.

## 1. Road

- Name and class: Gaisberg Höhenstraße = Landesstraße L108 [src: https://www.salzburg24.at/news/salzburg/stadt/chaos-am-gaisberg-120-autos-blockieren-gipfel-und-busverkehr-art-284538 ; search summary of austria-forum].
- Length: 8.6 km [src: https://austria-forum.org/af/AustriaWiki/Gaisberg_%28Salzburg%29].
- Opened 1929 (text) / 1928 (infobox) [src: austria-forum above].
- Width: unknown. Lane count: unknown (2 lanes, one per direction, visible in photos *(photo: road-gaisbergstrasse-hoehenstrasse-01.jpg was not visually checked)*; not confirmed).
- Surface: asphalt. The lower part was newly asphalted (per Quäldich, "neu asphaltiert") [src: quaeldich above]. Upper surface type: unknown.
- Centre line: dashed white centre line on the Gersberg-Alm section *(photo: road-gaisbergstrasse-hoehenstrasse-01.jpg)*. Edge line: solid white edge line, continuous, on the outside of the bend and the Gersberg section *(photos: road-hairpin-curve-wanderweg-sign-01.jpg, road-gaisbergstrasse-hoehenstrasse-01.jpg)*. Any painted words/arrows on the surface: unknown (one white arrow/chevron symbol seen on the Gersberg approach *(photo: road-gaisbergstrasse-hoehenstrasse-01.jpg)*).
- Guard rail: steel W-beam (Austrian "Leitschiene") on the outside of bends and along the road edge, galvanised grey, with posts. *(photos: road-hairpin-curve-wanderweg-sign-01.jpg, road-gaisbergstrasse-hoehenstrasse-01.jpg)*. Exact profile/standard: unknown.
- Roadside posts (Leitpflock): Austrian StVO §57 — reflector on the right in driving direction = red, on the left = white; yellow where passable from both sides [src: https://gesetzefinden.at/bundesrecht/bundesgesetze/stvo-1960/para-57 (search summary; wording in the search result)]. Spacing: unknown. Austrian RVS spacing value not found; the 50 m figure is German StVO, not Austrian [src: search summary, no Austrian source].
- Visible on road: white-and-red chevron boards on bends *(photo: road-hairpin-curve-wanderweg-sign-01.jpg)*. Red-white-striped hazard post at bend *(photo: road-hairpin-curve-wanderweg-sign-01.jpg)*.
- Signs seen: round red-ring bicycle prohibition sign with plate "Gilt für Wanderweg" (applies to the hiking path, not the road) *(photo: road-hairpin-curve-wanderweg-sign-01.jpg)*. Convex mirror on a post at a junction *(photo: road-gaisbergstrasse-hoehenstrasse-01.jpg)*. Give-way triangle at junction *(photo: road-gaisbergstrasse-hoehenstrasse-01.jpg)*.
- Speed limit: unknown.
- Hairpins: total count unknown.
  - Nocksteinkehre at ~1130 m [src: quaeldich].
  - Two long hairpins ("zwei langgezogene Kehren") in the last ~2.5 km above Zistelalm, unnamed [src: quaeldich].
  - Western approach from Aigen: a sixth hairpin, after which motor traffic is banned [src: quaeldich].
- Steepest sections:
  - Elsbethen approach, forest start: up to 13% [src: quaeldich].
  - Two straights above Nocksteinkehre: average 12% [src: quaeldich].
  - Aigen approach, ~500 m section: average over 16%, three very steep ramps [src: quaeldich].
  - Guggenthal approach near Gersbergalm: about 8% [src: quaeldich].
- Average gradient (per Quäldich): Elsbethen 7.6%, Guggenthal 6.8%, Wiestal 5.6%, Aigen 10.0% [src: quaeldich].
- Elevation gain (per Quäldich): Elsbethen 810 m, Guggenthal 600 m, Wiestal 756 m, Aigen 821 m [src: quaeldich].
- Gersbergalm level: 756 m; Zistelalm 980 m; Nocksteinkehre 1130 m; Gaisbergspitze 1265–1288 m [src: quaeldich].
- Bus stops: Zistelalm stop (line 151 terminus from 7 Oct 2024) [search summary, salzburg-ag / salzburg24]. Summit stop, Mirabellplatz start, bus operated by Albus [src: https://www.salzburg24.at/news/salzburg/stadt/chaos-am-gaisberg-120-autos-blockieren-gipfel-und-busverkehr-art-284538]. Other bus stops: unknown.
- Closures: 16 Oct 2022 combustion vehicles barred until 16:00, residents excepted [src: austria-forum]. Feb 2025: closure "ab der Zistelalm" was not enforced on a busy Sunday [src: salzburg24 art-284538]. Closures are called for when crowds are heavy [search summary, salzburg24 art-281753].
- Races: motorbike and car races on the road 1929–1968; historic-car event yearly since 2002; cycling race on the road since 2002 [src: austria-forum].

## 2. Vegetation by altitude

- Forest: beech and mixed forest on the lower slopes [search summary, ich-geh-wandern hike description]. Spruce (dark conifers) on the upper slopes and cliffs *(photos: road-hairpin-curve-wanderweg-sign-01.jpg, view-gaisberg-wooded-peak-transmitter-2018.jpg)*. Larch, fir, maple, ash: unknown.
- Forest vs meadow share: unknown (no source gave a percentage).
- Protected forest: small western cliff area (Kapaunwände, below the summit plateau) is the Naturwaldreservat Gaisberg [src: austria-forum].
- Hedges and single trees: single large trees along the Gersberg road section *(photo: road-gaisbergstrasse-hoehenstrasse-01.jpg)*. Roadside hedges: unknown.
- Meadows: pastures on Kühberg and Gersberg slopes (per Commons titles; mown vs grazed unknown) *(Commons titles only, not viewed: meadow-gersberg-pasture-01.jpg, meadow-kuehberg-autumn-01..04.jpg)*. Cows on the meadows: unknown (not seen in the photos I checked).
- Summit plateau: short grass with gravel parking area, a few large spruces, one stone-built structure *(photo: summit-plateau-meadow-buildings-01.jpg)*.
- Autumn colour: October 2021 Kühberg photos (meadow-kuehberg-autumn-01..04.jpg) are dated 4 Oct 2021 on Commons; not viewed, so no colour claim is made from them. Peak timing in October: unknown; exact dates not found.
- Snow: Dec 22–26 2024 and 12 Feb 2025 summit snow *(viewed: summit-snow-fence-view-dec2024.jpg; other Dec 2024 / Feb 2025 files are Commons titles only)*. First snow in October: unknown.
- Flora: flowering Teufelskralle (Phyteuma) on the Gaisberg meadow in June 2025 *(Commons title only, not viewed)*.

## 4. Summit

- Sender Gaisberg (transmitter): mast height 100 m [src: https://www.salzburg24.at/news/salzburg/stadt/radio-sendeanlage-am-gaisberg-ausgefallen-art-209297]. Commissioned 20 Dec 1956 [src: austria-forum]. Operator: ORS, carries ORF broadcasts [src: salzburg24 art-209297].
- Structure type (lattice / guyed / concrete): unknown. Colour: red-and-white banding visible on the mast seen from distance *(photo: view-gaisberg-wooded-peak-transmitter-2018.jpg)*; I did not confirm that photo shows the Gaisberg mast.
- Antennas: unknown. Other buildings: a stone-clad building with mesh fence and antennas on the summit plateau *(photo: summit-plateau-meadow-buildings-01.jpg)*.
- Lightning research: 40–50 strikes/year on the mast; Blitzforschungszentrum est. 1998–2000 [src: austria-forum]. Autumn 2020: about 100 GPS-timed geophones placed around the mast [src: austria-forum].
- Viewing platform: unknown.
- Parking: 55 designated spaces at the summit (since Oct 2009) [src: austria-forum; salzburg24 art-284538]. Nov 2024: 52 spaces counted [search summary, salzburg24 art-281753]. On 2 Feb 2025 about 120 cars plus bus [src: salzburg24 art-284538].
- Summit cross: present *(Commons titles only, not viewed)*.
- Information board: exists *(Commons title only, not viewed)*. Contents: unknown.
- Memorial stone (Eduard Kuhn) in the parking area *(Commons title only)*.
- Signage: parking signs and closure notices in the summit area (see photos summit-parking-01/02.jpg). Exact wording: unknown.
- Paraglider launch: three launch sites just below the summit; pilots land in a meadow in Aigen [src: austria-forum]. Gliding club field at ~1280 m [search summary, Salzburg Paragliding listing]. Tandem flights sold from the summit (meeting point Gaisbergspitze) [search summary, manawa.com]. Crowding: unknown.
- Busy periods: sunny weekends; road closures when cars exceed capacity [src: salzburg24 art-284538].

## 6. Life on the climb

- Cyclists: Quäldich says the road is used by road cyclists mainly April to October (84% of recorded rides) [src: quaeldich]. Ascent counts on the site: Elsbethen 792, Guggenthal 610, Wiestal 658, Aigen 913 (QDH values, per Quäldich).
- Motorists: cars on sunny weekends; 120 cars counted 2 Feb 2025 [src: salzburg24 art-284538].
- Buses: line 151 (Albus), Mirabellplatz to summit/Zistelalm [src: salzburg24 art-284538; austria-forum].
- Paragliders: launch and tandem flights from the summit [src: austria-forum; manawa.com].
- Hikers: multiple long-distance trails cross the summit [src: austria-forum]. Hiking route via Vorderfager car park, Glasenbach, beech forest, Rauchenbüchelhütte, Zistelalm [search summary].
- Cows and horses: unknown in sources; pastures visible in photos.
- Birds (alpine choughs etc.): unknown.
- Times of use: weekends and sunny days most crowded [src: salzburg24 art-284538].

## 7. Weather and light

- Inversion: fog and cloud lie over the Salzburg basin while the mountains (Gaisberg) are in sun. Reported in Oct 2025 and Nov 2020 [src: https://www.salzburg24.at/news/salzburg/nebel-im-tal-sonne-am-berg-wetterphaenomen-in-bildern-art-312317 ; https://www.salzburg24.at/news/salzburg/stadt/sonnenschein-lockt-salzburger-auf-gaisberg-art-234797 ; https://www.salzburg24.at/news/salzburg/warum-die-sonne-nur-am-berg-zu-sehen-ist-inversionswetterlage-einfach-erklaert-art-234913].
- Temperature example: about -5 °C in the city vs +7 °C on the Gaisberg on one December day [search summary, salzburg24].
- Föhn: unknown.
- October snow: unknown. Summit snow in Dec 2024 and Feb 2025 (photos).
- Light: clear sky over the summit on sunny days; hazy valleys in inversion conditions *(photo: inn-gersberg-alm-romantik-hotel-01.jpg, forested view down to the basin)*.
