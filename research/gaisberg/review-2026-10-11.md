# Review 2026-10-11: reversed depth buffer (perf pass)

Change: logarithmic depth buffer replaced by a reversed float depth buffer, with fixes for N8AO's reversed path and
the PCF shadow bias (see `docs/lessons.md`, Rendering). Meant to look identical. Frames: engine paused, `atmo.uTime`
fixed, HUD hidden, rendered with `?depth=log` (before) and without (after) at six spots. Mean absolute pixel
difference out of 255: 0.60, 0.66, 1.14, 0.80, 0.34, 0.46 (Formentor coast, checked separately: 0.92, 0.96).
Reviewer: serpentine-reviewer (Haiku). Report verbatim:

1. Gnigl start, chase, golden hour: same. Road, building walls, and shrubs match. No stripes or shadow changes.
2. Forest climb, chase: same. Road edge, hillside, and tree bases match.
3. Drone over switchbacks: same at this resolution. Foliage and road edges match. The largest measured difference (1.14) is most likely antialiasing in the canopy, which I cannot resolve visually.
4. Upper climb, noon: same. Grass, road, and the distant house match. No acne or missing geometry.
5. Summit, side camera, fog inversion: same. Valley fog, ground, and rider shadow match.
6. Summit drone, dusk: same. Road, buildings, figures, and distant ridges match. No z-fighting on the ridge or road.

Blockers: none. I saw no z-fighting, flicker stripes, shadow acne, missing or cut-off geometry, black or NaN patches, or haze or fog changes in any pair.

Verdict: APPROVE. All six pairs look identical to the eye, and the largest measured difference (1.14) is consistent with antialiasing noise in the forest canopy.

Limitation: this is a visual judgment on the images as viewed. I did not run a pixel diff, and I did not zoom into the canopy or the road-ground seams.
