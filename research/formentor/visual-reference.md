## 1. Road

### 1.1 Length and start point
- "The 13.5 km road which runs from Port de Pollença to Cap de Formentor" was built in 1925 by engineer Antonio Parietti. Source: UH-2025 (https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/part-forana/2025/06/07/2403203/este-acantilado-mas-alto-mallorca-384-metros-altura-sobre-nivel-del-mar.html)
- "Formentor Beach is located about 14 km from Puerto de Pollensa." Source: CLICK-BUS.
- BBR gives 17 km for the Ma-2210 "Can Sigala - Cap de Formentor" section.
- SEEM says "18km of winding roads, hairpin bends, tunnels, cliffs, breathtaking views and wilderness stretch along the Ma2210."
- A search summary gave "Between kilometer 8.7 and 19,600" as the controlled stretch (CLICK-BUS, ARAB). Km 8.7 is the checkpoint, and the lighthouse section is controlled up to km 19.6. Sources use different zero points, so the total is **uncertain between about 13.5 and 19.6 km**.
- Lighthouse: "a 15-kilometre winding road" (BCN-GUIDE). Use 15 km as the working figure for the lighthouse end.

### 1.7 Climb profile (sequence)
1. Port de Pollença start (sea level).
2. Climb past the Talaia d'Albercutx turn-off (see §3.2) on the outbound road.
3. Mirador de Sa Creueta / Mirador des Colomer viewpoint (cliff edge, §3.1).
4. Descent to Platja de Formentor (§4). Beach "about 14 km" from Pollença (CLICK-BUS).
5. Last section toward the lighthouse, "10+ km largely traffic-free" for cyclists (MDB-2026). BBR: "climb once again up the cliffs of the cape."
6. Lighthouse at the tip (§5).

Exact metre values for each climb are **unknown**.

### 1.9 Road closure and access rules (2026)
- Dates: "restrictions apply from 15 May until 18 October 2026, daily between 10am and 10pm." (MDB-2026). Other 2026 articles give 15 October as the end (CLICK-BUS, PALMA-AIR-2026). Treat 18 October as the newer date.
- Outside 10:00–22:00 the road is open to cars (ARAB, MDB-2026).
- Zone 1: Port de Pollença roundabout to Formentor beach. Private cars enter only while the beach car park has space; a barrier closes when full. Roadside parking is not permitted (ARAB).
- Zone 2: beach to lighthouse. "Only residents, permit holders and public transport may pass the checkpoint at kilometre 8.7." (MDB-2026). Beyond the barrier, access is for authorised vehicles, mainly those serving the lighthouse bar, plus cyclists, hikers and goats (MDB-2026).
- Possible review ahead of the 12 August 2026 solar eclipse (MDB-2026).
- Fines: "€100 to €200" (MDB-2026). CLICK-BUS gives about €80 for entering without a ticket. The sources differ; the MDB figure is more recent.

### 1.10 Shuttle bus
- TIB line 334 is "the only public transport exception and carries most visitors" (MDB-2026).
- Timings: "from 9:30 a.m. to 9:50 p.m., and there are departures every 35 minutes." Fare: €1.80 to €3. Stops: Alcúdia, Port de Pollença, Formentor Beach, Cala Figuera / Cala Murta, Formentor Lighthouse. Coves served June to September only. (CLICK-BUS)
- Bus stop between beach and lighthouse: "Cala Murta-Cala Figuera is the only bus stop between the beach and the lighthouse." (MDB-2026)

## 3. Mirador es Colomer (Mirador de Mal Pas) and Talaia d'Albercutx

### 3.1 Mirador
- Names: "Mirador de Sa Creueta" (SEEM), "Mirador des Colomer" (MDB-2026), "Mirador del Mal Pas, also named Mirador d'es Colomer after the small island Colomer" (UH-2025).
- Stone walkway: "At Mirador de Sa Creueta, a stone walkway offers the breathtaking views, and it is built right at the edge of the sheer cliff" (SEEM). Source: https://www.seemallorca.com/reviews/a-day-trip-to-formentor-formentor-685878
- Islet: "little Illot de Colomer perched off the end of the rocks" (SEEM). Also called "Illa des Colomer" in some sources.
- Cliff height at the mirador: a search summary gave "more than 200 metres high" (search summary; not confirmed on a fetched page). UH-2025 says "sea roars 300m below" the viewpoints.
- Stairs, railings, parking area: parking nearby (search summary, MDB-2026 mentions heavy visitor numbers at Mirador des Colomer). Stairs and railings: **unknown.**
- Platform material: stone (SEEM). Platform size: **unknown.**

### 3.2 Talaia d'Albercutx
- Height: **380 m** (LP, "watchtower (380m)"). Single source; not verified on a second page. Wikipedia (es) was not retrieved (HTTP 404).

- Access: "a rough side road" opposite the viewpoint (search summary); "a couple of kilometers up a side road from the Mirador de Sa Creueta" (LP). Gradient: **unknown.**

### 4.2 Hotel
- Opened: 24 August 1929 (FS-DE: "When the hotel opened on August 24, 1929, guests arrived by boat because the road ... was not finished until six months later.")
- Style: "neoclassical and Art Deco elements" (Mallorcan newspaper, search summary). FS-DE (German page) says the architecture was influenced by modernist design and created a simple "white line in the landscape."
- Architect: **unknown.** Sources conflict. MELIA (search summary) credits "Bartolomé Meliá" via a generic travel site; treat as unverified.

- Renovations: 2021 by Estudio Lamela; part of the building gutted, leaving exterior walls (search summary, MDB). 2024 by Gilles&Boissier (search summary).
- Parking: "Formentor Lighthouse Car Park, and Platjamar A and B," named legal car parks (CLICK-BUS). Price "€0.064 per minute, which is equivalent to almost €4 per hour." Open 24 hours.

## 5. Last section and lighthouse

### 5.1 Lighthouse (Far de Formentor)
- Built: first lit **1863** (WIKI-LH infobox, "Lighthouses completed in 1863"). Construction began 1857 (BCN-GUIDE).
- Tower height: **22 m (72 ft)** (WIKI-LH).
- Focal height above sea: **210 m (690 ft)**, "the highest lighthouse in the Balearic Islands" (WIKI-LH).
- Colours: "white tower and buildings, grey lantern" (WIKI-LH). The tower is masonry, cylindrical, with a double balcony and a lantern.
- Light characteristic: Fl(4) W 20s; range 24 nautical miles (WIKI-LH).
- ROIG gives a different figure: "the tower stands 56 metres tall" and the site "rises some 188 metres above sea level." Treat as the less reliable figure (ROIG, https://roig.com/en/?p=8295).
- Keeper's house: **unknown.**
- Café: "a large squat, square building which serves as a souvenir shop and café." (SEEM). Café "underneath the lighthouse" (BBR). This is the building the lighthouse road ends at.
- Terrace: "the extensive and once again stunning view" of the sea (SEEM).

### 5.2 Cliffs
- "Cap de Formentor has 400m high cliffs, sheer drops and azure waters." (SEEM)
- Cliff height at the lighthouse: 210 m focal height (WIKI-LH). Sea 300 m below viewpoints (UH-2025).
- Narrow road with walls: **unknown.**

### 5.3 Sa Creueta / viewpoint on the last stretch
- Mirador de Sa Creueta is on the outbound climb, not the last stretch (SEEM, §3.1).
- Viewpoints on the last stretch: "unbroken sea views on both sides" over the 11 km toward the lighthouse (SEEM).
- Famous ridge view of the road snaking along the cliff: **unknown** (not described in any source).

### 8.2 Cyclists
- Authorised users during restrictions (MDB-2026). Peak season and counts: **unknown.**

### 8.3 Cars and buses
- Cars: restricted 10:00–22:00 (§1.9). Permit holders and residents allowed (MDB-2026).
- Buses: TIB 334 every 35 minutes (CLICK-BUS, MDB-2026). The bus "tackles" the final approach (MDB-2026).

### 8.4 Birds
- Eleonora's falcon: breeding colony on the peninsula; "this area is famous for its windswept lighthouse on the promontory ... observe Eleonora's Falcons nesting on the cliffs." (search summary, BIRDFORUM, https://www.birdforum.net/opus/Formentor_Peninsula)
- Cliff birds: Blue Rock Thrush, Rock Dove, Northern Raven, Eurasian Crag Martin (search summary, BIRDFORUM).
- Balearic Warbler on cliffs and scrub (search summary).
- Black vultures: **unknown** (not found).
- Gulls: **unknown** (not found).

### 8.5 Boats offshore

## 10. Facts that most help the 3D scene

1. Road is one ribbon of climbs and zigzags with a checkpoint at km 8.7 and cars banned 10:00–22:00 until 18 October 2026, only buses, permit holders, cyclists, hikers and goats past the barrier (MDB-2026).
2. Lighthouse: white masonry tower, grey lantern, 22 m tall, focal height 210 m, built 1863 (WIKI-LH).
3. Cliff-edge stone walkway at Mirador de Sa Creueta with the islet Illot des Colomer below (SEEM, UH-2025).
4. Cap de Formentor cliffs: 400 m, sea about 300 m below the viewpoints (SEEM, UH-2025).
5. Pine forest (Aleppo pine) lining both sides of the road and the beach (MDB-2021-PINE, CLICK-BUS).
6. Hotel Formentor: white building, opened 1929; architecture neoclassical and Art Deco per local press (FS-DE, search summary).
7. Goats are a real presence beyond the barrier and at the lighthouse (SEEM, MDB-2026).
8. Eleonora's falcon colony on the cliffs; cliff birds include crag martin and raven (BIRDFORUM).
9. Azure water and clear-day views of Menorca to the east (SEEM, BBR, UH-2025).
10. Talaia d'Albercutx: old watchtower at 380 m, reached by a side road and metal ladder (LP, SEEM).
